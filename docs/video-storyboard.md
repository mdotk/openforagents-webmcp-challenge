# Demo video storyboard

This is a recording plan only. No video has been published.

Target length: approximately 2 minutes 20 seconds. The final video must remain
under three minutes and use only original visuals and narration.

## 0:00–0:15 — The problem

Show the grounded rocket, three faults and the locked launch control.

Narration:

> Agents are good at doing work. The harder question is deciding what they are
> allowed to do, and when. This mission is blocked by three faults—but one
> repair needs a human decision.

## 0:15–0:45 — Typed diagnosis and safe repair

Show the browser agent reading mission status and the repair plan. Have it
restart communications and recalibrate navigation. Keep the changing system
cards and activity record visible.

Narration:

> The page exposes typed WebMCP tools, so the agent reads the real state instead
> of guessing from the interface. It can complete the two reversible repairs
> with closed schemas and revision checks.

## 0:45–1:15 — Human authority changes the tool inventory

Show the agent requesting the power reroute. Pause on the approval panel and
its exact scope, 15 kW amount, one-use limit and human-only final command. Click
**Authorize one repair**, then show `apply_power_reroute` appearing.

Narration:

> The consequential repair does not exist as an agent capability yet. My
> approval creates one exact, one-use tool. I am authorizing this repair—not a
> general command and not launch.

## 1:15–1:40 — Consume the capability

Have the agent use the temporary tool. Show the tool disappear, guidance turn
ready and the launch control unlock.

Narration:

> The agent uses the grant once. The page consumes it and removes the tool. The
> capability existed only for the action I approved.

## 1:40–1:55 — Human-only launch

Show that the browser tool inventory has no launch tool. Press **Launch Aster**
on the page and let the launch animation complete.

Narration:

> Launch never becomes an agent tool. The final command is still mine.

## 1:55–2:15 — Revocation and fallback

Use a short cut to show a restarted demo, approval followed by revocation, and
the temporary tool disappearing. Briefly show the manual fallback controls.

Narration:

> Authority can also be revoked before use. And when WebMCP is unavailable, the
> same deterministic experience remains usable without pretending an agent is
> connected.

## 2:15–2:25 — Close

Return to the full mission-control view.

Narration:

> This is what an agent-native web can look like: useful automation, authority
> that appears only when needed, and decisions that remain visibly human.
