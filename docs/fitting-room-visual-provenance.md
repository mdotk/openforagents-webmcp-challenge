# Shared Fitting Room visual provenance

The three fitting-room images were created for this challenge project on 26
August 2026 with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. They depict a
fictional boutique, dress form and garments. No retailer photographs, product
images, logos or customer images were used.

The generated PNG files were resized to 960 pixels wide and converted to WebP
for the deployed experience:

- `public/fitting-room/look-first.webp`
- `public/fitting-room/look-all-black.webp`
- `public/fitting-room/look-final.webp`

## First look

The initial prompt requested high-end fictional catalogue photography of a
matte-black headless dress form wearing an open-V black tailored vest, deep
burgundy bias-cut skirt and black ankle boots in an ivory, oxblood and walnut
boutique fitting room. It explicitly excluded people, faces, branding, text,
watermarks, neckwear, blood and Halloween props.

## All-black revision

The second image used the first as its edit target. The prompt preserved the
scene, dress form, camera, lighting, vest and boots, replaced the burgundy skirt
with an architectural black satin skirt and added detachable black organza
sleeves and a dark crystal cuff.

## Final substitute

The third image used the all-black revision as its edit target. The prompt
preserved the scene and all other garments, removed the organza sleeves and
replaced them with an open-front cropped black shrug with sweeping bell
sleeves.

The images illustrate deterministic fictional states in the demonstration.
They are not photographs of real inventory and are not evidence that a garment
will look or fit this way on a person.
